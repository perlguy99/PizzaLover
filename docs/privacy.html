Pizza Lover

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

Pizza Lover is built to work entirely with your own data, synced through your own iCloud account. There is no developer-owned server, no account system, and no third-party analytics or advertising in this app.

What the app stores

  • Pizza ratings: the crust, sauce, toppings, and overall taste scores you give each pizza, along with the date and any notes you write.
  • Photos: if you attach a photo to a pizza entry, it's stored with that entry.
  • Restaurant info: name, address, and phone number for places you've logged a pizza at or marked as a favorite, sourced from Apple Maps (MapKit) search results.

All of this data is stored using Apple's CloudKit, inside your own private iCloud account. It syncs automatically across your own devices signed into the same Apple ID. The developer cannot see, access, or download this data — it never passes through a server we operate.

Location

The app asks for your location only to find nearby pizza restaurants (the Nearby and Random Pizza features), using Apple's MapKit. Your location is not stored, logged, or shared — it's used live, in the moment, to run that search.

Camera and Photo Library

If you choose to attach a photo to a pizza entry, the app accesses your camera or photo library only for that purpose, only when you tap to do so. Photos you select are stored the same way as the rest of your data — in your own iCloud, via CloudKit.

What the app does NOT do

  • No advertising, no ad networks
  • No third-party analytics or tracking SDKs
  • No account creation, login, or password
  • No data sold or shared with third parties
  • No data collected beyond what's described above

Data deletion

Since all data lives in your own iCloud account, you can delete it at any time by deleting entries within the app, or by removing the app's iCloud data via iOS Settings → [Your Name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage.
